# Limitations: Target Scope, Source Drift, and Missing Evidence {#sec:limitations}

The ledger is a declared {{TARGET_TOTAL}}-target model, not a complete bibliography or a worldwide census of Blake holdings. It represents {{TARGETS_REPRESENTED}} targets and fully satisfies evidence for {{TARGETS_PRESENT}}. Partial, missing, duplicate, and unverified states remain explicit. No result establishes a completed Blake corpus [@biber1993representativeness; @pechenick2015googlebooks; @bode2018world].

Local records have source/copy granularity. They are not independent works; image associations are not independent physical files. The {{WORKS_WITH_TEXT}} text-bearing records include primary and unverified reading roles. Repeated copies, edition apparatus, captions, transcription errors, and unknown authorship can affect aggregate words, vocabulary, entities, themes, sentiment, and projections. Private primary-scope occurrence search has stricter authorship rules than the exploratory NLP population.

The image inventory is bounded by discovered identifiers and successful local capture. Recovery leaves {{RECOVERY_UNRESOLVED_IMAGES}} discovered image identifiers unresolved. Copy, plate, impression, coloring, unpublished-source, and museum-object coverage require item-level scholarship beyond this inventory [@viscomi1993blake; @essick1980printmaker; @butlin1981paintings; @phillips2000songs]. Nominal source resolution codes do not independently establish scan resolution or image quality.

This analysis reused local material without refreshing discovery, acquisition, or endpoint health. Retained URLs, source commits, and historical checks do not establish present remote availability. The search queue is a review aid, not a guarantee that a candidate contains the required text or image. Morgan manuscript pages and scholarly bibliographies can guide review without satisfying whole-work evidence [@morgan_pickering; @bq_fourzoas_bibliography; @yale_blake_collections; @reed2014managing; @klein2014reference].

The models have no task-specific accuracy validation here. VADER sentiment, heuristic readability, weighted-input topic extraction, spaCy entities, rule-based themes, and image descriptors are computational diagnostics rather than literary judgments. Short-text and pruned-vocabulary failures remain unavailable components. The principal-component display is vocabulary- and membership-dependent and is evaluated in floating-point arithmetic. A numerical oracle checks computation, not literary validity [@flesch1948new; @kimklinger2019sentiment; @blei2012topic; @ramsay2011readingmachines].

The Python dependency lock does not pin separately downloaded language-resource bytes or guarantee bitwise equality of a new model run. Saved result ledgers and their identities define the numerical evidence used by this publication.

Preservation evidence is also bounded. Checksums and the restore drill test captured bytes; they do not prove transcription correctness, complete source acquisition, scholarly acceptance, or redistribution rights. The backup and restore copies reside locally, and a copied-byte drill alone does not establish resilience to loss of the entire physical storage device. The public release omits expressive provider files and image mosaics. Its code license does not relicense the private evidence cache.

The generated cover is a labeled design asset with recorded provenance, outside the corpus ledger. Generative images supply no evidence about Blake's objects or historical technique [@klein2025provocations].
